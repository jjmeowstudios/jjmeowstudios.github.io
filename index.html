GET TO KNOW BRAINBLOQ

Your questions, answered.

What is BrainBloq?

BrainBloq is a block puzzle game by JJ Meow Studios for Android and iOS. It has three game modes: Classic, Adventure and Puzzle.

How do you play Classic mode?

Drag pieces onto the board and complete rows and columns to clear blocks and score points. Chain combos and try to beat your personal best.

What does Adventure mode offer?

In Adventure, you complete level goals: score points, clear colored blocks, collect gems and diamonds or uncover hidden objects. Challenges are organized into seasons.

How does Puzzle mode work?

In Puzzle, work out how to fit the pieces on each board while accounting for obstacles. Choose from five difficulty levels and use hints when you need them.

Is BrainBloq free and where can I download it?

BrainBloq is free to download on the App Store for iOS and Google Play for Android. You can find both download links on this page.
